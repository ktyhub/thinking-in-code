Release cadence

mlflow moves faster: 8 releases in the last 90 days against 3 for crawl4ai.

Measured by the gap between consecutive releases, the median for mlflow is 13 days versus 20 days for crawl4ai. Shorter gaps make upgrade windows harder to plan — but they also mean fixes land sooner.

How to choose

You need features to keep landing
Pick mlflow. 8 releases in 90 days with a 13 days median gap means the API surface keeps moving — your docs and clients have to keep up.
You want a predictable upgrade cycle
Pick crawl4ai. A 20 days median gap and 3 releases in 90 days mean fewer upgrade batches and a narrower regression surface.
You only ship stable builds
mlflow carries 0 prerelease builds, crawl4ai 0. Counting stable releases only, the usable totals are 11 and 20.
