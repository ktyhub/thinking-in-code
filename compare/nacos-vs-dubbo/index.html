Release cadence

nacos moves faster: 4 releases in the last 90 days against 1 for dubbo.

Measured by the gap between consecutive releases, the median for nacos is 33 days versus 27 days for dubbo. Shorter gaps make upgrade windows harder to plan — but they also mean fixes land sooner.

How to choose

You need features to keep landing
Pick nacos. 4 releases in 90 days with a 33 days median gap means the API surface keeps moving — your docs and clients have to keep up.
You want a predictable upgrade cycle
Pick dubbo. A 27 days median gap and 1 releases in 90 days mean fewer upgrade batches and a narrower regression surface.
You only ship stable builds
nacos carries 0 prerelease builds, dubbo 4. Counting stable releases only, the usable totals are 25 and 16.
