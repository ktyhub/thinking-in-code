Stirling-PDF vs OCRmyPDF

Stirling-PDF and OCRmyPDF are both tracked here. Stirling-PDF has 30 indexed releases, the latest being v3.3.0 on Nov 5, 2026; OCRmyPDF has 11, the latest v17.14.2 on Nov 16, 2026. The comparison below is built from their actual release cadence and channel mix.

Release cadence

Stirling-PDF moves faster: 8 releases in the last 90 days against 7 for OCRmyPDF.

Measured by the gap between consecutive releases, the median for Stirling-PDF is 6 days versus 12 days for OCRmyPDF. Shorter gaps make upgrade windows harder to plan — but they also mean fixes land sooner.

How to choose

You need features to keep landing
Pick Stirling-PDF. 8 releases in 90 days with a 6 days median gap means the API surface keeps moving — your docs and clients have to keep up.
You want a predictable upgrade cycle
Pick OCRmyPDF. A 12 days median gap and 7 releases in 90 days mean fewer upgrade batches and a narrower regression surface.
You only ship stable builds
Stirling-PDF carries 0 prerelease builds, OCRmyPDF 0. Counting stable releases only, the usable totals are 30 and 11.
