Release cadence

ms-swift moves faster: 3 releases in the last 90 days against 0 for outlines.

Measured by the gap between consecutive releases, the median for ms-swift is 12 days versus 20 days for outlines. Shorter gaps make upgrade windows harder to plan — but they also mean fixes land sooner.

How to choose

You need features to keep landing
Pick ms-swift. 3 releases in 90 days with a 12 days median gap means the API surface keeps moving — your docs and clients have to keep up.
You want a predictable upgrade cycle
Pick outlines. A 20 days median gap and 0 releases in 90 days mean fewer upgrade batches and a narrower regression surface.
