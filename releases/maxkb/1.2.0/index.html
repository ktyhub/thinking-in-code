MaxKB v1.2.0

minor PublishedMay 30, 2024 Source1Panel-dev/MaxKB Chinese translation

MaxKB v1.2.0

新增功能：

  • 【知识库】支持上传 Excel/CSV 格式的 QA 问答对，自动关联问题。 by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/523
  • 【知识库】支持上传 HTML 格式的文本文件。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/518
  • 【知识库】支持知识库和文档重新向量化操作。by @wangdan-fit2cloud in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/490
  • 【知识库】支持导出知识库和文档。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/553
  • 【问答页面】支持新建对话和查看历史对话。by @wangdan-fit2cloud in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/485
  • 【问答页面】支持导出对话记录。by @youngzm339 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/502

功能优化：

  • 【应用】显示知识来源的引用分段，支持 markdown 样式显示。by @wangdan-fit2cloud in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/491
  • 【应用】提问后模型返回异常信息时也要记录到对话日志中。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/486
  • 【应用】在对话日志详情中修正答案时，问题可以修改。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/527
  • 【应用】引用分段数最大值调整为 100 。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/580
  • 【应用】开场白最大字符数调整为 4096 。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/526

问题修复：

  • 【应用】解决开场白中无法显示 markdown 样式的表格。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/511
  • 【问答页面】解决提问时出现【用户问题】此字段不能为空字符串的问题。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/557
  • 【知识库】修复删除最后一个分段时报错的问题。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/534
  • 【知识库】上传 md格式文档，一个分段内容超过 4096 个字符时，上传报错。by @shaohuzhang1 in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/577
  • 【关于】修复刷新浏览器后不显示版本号的问题。by @wangdan-fit2cloud in https://github.com/1Panel-dev/MaxKB/pull/550
