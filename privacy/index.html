Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What this site is

This is a static, public website that mirrors open-source release notes and publishes original analysis of release cadence. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no comment forms and no payment flows. We do not ask you to submit personal information.

Information we collect

We do not run our own tracking database. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and delivery purposes. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, non-identifying usage such as which pages are visited.

Cookies and third-party advertising

We use Google AdSense to display a small number of advertisements at the end of article pages. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show ads that may be relevant to you.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data on its partner sites at Google's privacy policy page. You can also disable or delete cookies in your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from reading any content on this site.

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Google — How ads use data
  • Google Privacy Policy

Third-party ad servers and networks may use their own technologies and are governed by their own privacy policies. We have no access to, and no control over, these cookies.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, because we do not collect any. Content on this site is drawn from public upstream repositories; release notes remain the property of their respective projects.

Children's privacy

This site is a technical reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the site's features change. If you have questions about this policy, or about how the site handles data, you can reach us by email.
